# Lecture 15 Task — Present an AI-Agent Business Idea

Present the Week 14 assignment idea to the class. The presentation should explain how AI agents could support a business or software service.

## Presentation Checklist

- [ ] Introduce the business idea clearly.
- [ ] Identify the target audience.
- [ ] Explain the problem faced by that audience.
- [ ] Describe where the required data would come from and why it is appropriate.
- [ ] Show the agent workflow from input to result.
- [ ] Explain how the idea creates value.
- [ ] Mention privacy, source permissions, and human approval.
- [ ] Be ready to answer questions and receive feedback.

## My Presentation

My presentation used the JobPilot AI idea from the Week 14 assignment. The focus was on helping students and early-career applicants organize opportunities and prepare truthful, user-approved application materials.
