# Lecture 15 — Assignment Idea Presentations

## Overview

Lecture 15 was a presentation session. Each student presented the business idea prepared for the Week 14 assignment and explained how AI agents could help that business grow.

My presentation was based on **JobPilot AI**, a proposed service that helps students and early-career applicants organize job searches, evaluate job fit, prepare truthful application materials, and track applications while keeping the user in control of every outgoing message.

## Topics Covered

- Presenting an AI-agent business idea
- Explaining a target audience and its problem
- Identifying permitted and useful data sources
- Describing an agent workflow
- Explaining human review, privacy, and responsible use
- Receiving classroom feedback on an idea
