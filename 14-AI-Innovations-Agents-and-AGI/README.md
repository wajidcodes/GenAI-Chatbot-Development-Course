# Lecture 14 — AI Innovations, Agents & AGI

## Overview

This lecture explores developments in AI, newer models and agents, the Muse agent discussed in class, and Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). The class context noted that Muse was not available in Pakistan at the time of the lecture. The central assignment asks how AI agents could help grow a business.

## Topics Covered

- New developments in AI models and agent systems
- How AI agents use tools and complete multi-step tasks
- Muse, as discussed in class (availability noted in the class context)
- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), its meaning and open questions
- Responsible ways to apply agents to business workflows
